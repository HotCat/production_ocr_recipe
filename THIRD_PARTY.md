Original license files and copyright headers remain with the imported sources.
No single new license is applied over the entire collection.

- Each application retains its original `LICENSE`.
- Haskell dear-imgui: BSD-3-Clause, `dependencies/dear-imgui/LICENSE`.
- Dear ImGui: MIT, `dependencies/dear-imgui/imgui/LICENSE.txt`.
- ImGuizmo: MIT, `dependencies/dear-imgui/ImGuizmo/LICENSE`.
- ImPlot: MIT, `dependencies/dear-imgui/implot-0.16/LICENSE`.
- GLM: `dependencies/glm/copying.txt`.
- Haskell OpenCV: BSD-3-Clause, `dependencies/haskell-opencv/LICENSE`.
- Husk Scheme: MIT, `dependencies/husk-scheme/LICENSE`.
- modbuspp: upstream license in `dependencies/modbuspp/license` (GPLv3).
- Native projector/codec/utility sources preserve their original headers,
  including the TI/hidapi notices in `projector/LC4500API`.
- The project-specific reconstruction/motion/camera wrapper modifications are
  imported from the user's working copies. Existing author headers are retained.
- Fonts and texture/logo assets are inherited runtime resources. No ownership
  transfer or relicensing of those assets is implied by this source import.

Native OpenCV/Open3D/DIPlib/CGAL/Ceres, vendor camera/motion SDKs and Python/Paddle
packages are installed separately under their own licenses. Generated release
installers may contain vendor runtime components; they are deliberately ignored
by this source repository.

Upstream identities and local snapshot information are in
`dependencies/README.md` and `docs/import-manifest.json`.
